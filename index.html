06 About

      
Quality‑minded,
fast to learn.

    

    

      

        I trained as a data scientist, but what I care about most is reliable software. In about eighteen months
        I went from RAG chatbots, to agentic test generation, to the Model Context Protocol on client projects.
        I learn tools fast, and I like proving whether they help with a number rather than a demo.
      

      

        I'm happiest on cross-functional teams that apply AI to real engineering workflows and leave room to
        propose, prototype and ship. I also love explaining this stuff: I taught AI workshops to students in Vietnam,
        and I work comfortably in Arabic, English and French.
